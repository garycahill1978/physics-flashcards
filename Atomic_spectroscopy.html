What is real? Selected visible wavelengths are based on published atomic line data. Some entries involve different ionic states (for example Ca II) or forbidden auroral oxygen lines; a discharge lamp will not display every listed line. Except for hydrogen, the energy diagrams display independent illustrative transition pairs; they do not give complete or physically positioned term levels. The observed line-count build-up, relative line strengths, ionisation balance, collision rates, level populations and photon animations are deliberately simplified. Electrons stop in the discharge chamber; the spectrometer records illustrated arriving photons. These are not measured or predicted lamp/stellar spectra; all elements show selected transitions, not complete spectra. Absorption is an illustrative excited-state population model, not a prediction that every listed emission line absorbs strongly in a cool gas. Spectral line widths use a Gaussian instrumental profile (FWHM ≈ λ/R), with a minimum onscreen width to avoid disappearing at limited pixel resolution.
