Important: these are two different physical models.

At room temperature, atoms move hundreds of metres per second, yet a blackbody emits almost entirely infrared radiation. Molecular speed does not set photon colour.

Models and equations: monatomic ideal gas; Maxwell–Boltzmann distribution in three dimensions; Planck's spectral radiance law, Wien's displacement law and the Stefan–Boltzmann law. A dilute gas is not necessarily a blackbody; gas excitation, emission lines, dissociation and ionisation are deliberately excluded. All calculations run locally in this HTML file. Space = pause/resume.
